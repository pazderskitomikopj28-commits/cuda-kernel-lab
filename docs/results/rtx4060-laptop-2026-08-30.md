The first fresh-process sample shows the largest clock/ramp-up effect, especially
in the baseline kernels. The median is therefore the headline value; the P95 is
retained instead of discarding the cold sample.

The small bank-conflict delta is an observed result, not a claim that padding is
unimportant: on this input the tiled implementation is already dominated by
other memory-system effects. Nsight Compute metrics would be needed to attribute
the remaining gap to bank conflicts quantitatively.
